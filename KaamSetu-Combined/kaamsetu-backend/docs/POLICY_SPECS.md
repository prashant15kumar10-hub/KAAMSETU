# Draft policy specs — NOT ACTIVE. Approve (or change) before anything is built.

## A. Fair 2+1 selection
Objective preserved: contractor picks 2, KAAMSETU proposes an **eligible** third worker with fewer opportunities.
Open decisions:
1. **Slots:** jobs of 1–2 workers (suggest: no fairness slot) ; 4+ (suggest: one fairness slot per full group of three, remainder contractor-chosen).
2. **Eligibility floor:** required skill, location radius, availability on the date, worker consent to be proposed. Never lowered to fill the slot.
3. **Experience:** self-declared vs verified must be labelled; "unrated" ≠ "unqualified".
4. **"Fewer opportunities":** suggest counting accepted bookings in the last N days (N to decide); not ratings, not disputes.
5. **Tie-break / anti-gaming:** random among ties with a stored seed; cap proposals per worker per week; ignore bookings cancelled within X hours to stop farming.
6. **Rejection:** contractor may reject for a job-related reason (pick from a list + optional note) and receive one more eligible proposal; log every rejection.
7. **Patterns:** flag a contractor rejecting > Y% of unrated proposals for human review — flag, never auto-penalize.
8. **Appeals:** worker/contractor can challenge a record; no automatic blacklist from one event.
9. **No eligible third:** say so plainly; contractor proceeds with own choice.
10. **Pilot:** off by default, per-contractor opt-in, clearly labelled, measured (opportunities reaching under-selected workers, fill time, rejection rate).

## B. Cancellation & travel compensation
Needs decisions on: stages (before travel / in transit / after arrival / after work began), contractor no-show, distance source
(estimate only; label uncertainty), transport rate and cap, evidence (proportionate, no continuous tracking), exceptions, appeals,
and keeping travel compensation **separate** from earned wages. The estimate must be shown **before** the worker agrees.
Money movement is out of scope without a provider.

## C. Advances
Record: amount, date, purpose, who reported, worker acknowledge/disagree, correction history, reconciliation against wages.
Rules to approve: no unconfirmed advance shown as verified debt; no double deduction; no deductions outside agreed/lawful terms; legal review of Indian wage-deduction rules.

## D. Confidential safety reporting
Needs: a separate restricted table/role, no exposure in participant-visible fields, who can read (named roles), realistic
confidentiality limits, escalation route that actually exists (staffed). Until then the API refuses `safety` help with a pointer to 112.
